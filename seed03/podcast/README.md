# The Body, Inc. — Podcast Production Pack

This folder contains companion materials for a solo long-form episode inspired by Seed 03 — The Body, Inc.

- `podcast-script.md` — spoken working draft (approx. 22–28 minutes)
- `cue-cards.md` — ten glanceable recording cue cards and fact-check strip
- `research-notes.md` — source list, fact notes, and recording-day verification reminders

The materials are working drafts. Please re-check fast-moving regulatory claims before recording. The Seed 03 essay itself is unchanged.

## Editorial premise

The body as biology, the body as business, and our growing relationship with measurement: longevity science versus longevity marketing; women's health and research gaps; wearables; peptides and evidence; access; and the pressure to turn curiosity into content.

## Terminology note

The script explains the 2026 global name change from PCOS to polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome (PMOS) while acknowledging that PCOS remains the widely recognised older term.
